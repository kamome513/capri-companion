**Windows:** download `Capri-Setup-1.37.1.exe` below and run it. If you have 1.36 or newer, Capri updates herself. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed).

**Linux:** download `Capri-x86_64.AppImage` below, make it executable (right-click → Properties → Permissions → *Allow executing file as program*), and double-click it. If you have 1.37.0, it updates itself.

**New in 1.37.1: Ollama sets itself up.** No terminal needed:
- Settings → Assistant → AI → **Set up Ollama for me**, or just say **"Hey Capri, set up your brain"**.
- She installs Ollama, downloads a chat model that suits your PC (the small one on PCs with 8 GB of RAM or less), and switches her chat to it. About 3 GB, a few minutes, free, and it runs on your PC.
- Windows: fully automatic. Linux: a terminal opens for Ollama's installer because it needs your password, then she carries on by herself.
- Already have Ollama with a model? She just uses it.
