**Windows:** download `Capri-Setup-1.38.0.exe` below and run it. If you have 1.36 or newer, Capri updates herself. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed).

**Linux:** download `Capri-x86_64.AppImage` below, make it executable (right-click → Properties → Permissions → *Allow executing file as program*), and double-click it. If you have 1.37.0 or newer, it updates itself.

**Capri's Discord status follows what you're doing, and she's animated.** Energy runs through her shard on your profile, and the status changes:
- **Plugged in** on your desktop.
- **Backseat gaming** while you play, with the game's name if it's in your Steam library, and an animated controller badge.
- **Vibing to music** while Spotify, Apple Music, another music app or a music site (YouTube Music, SoundCloud...) plays, with an animated equaliser badge. It never shows which song.
- Your friends see the **Get Capri** and **Join her Discord** buttons. Discord hides your own buttons from you.
- Turn it off: Settings → PC & chat → Discord. Discord's own Activity Privacy setting also applies.
