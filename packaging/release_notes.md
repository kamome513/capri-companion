**Windows:** download `Capri-Setup-1.40.0.exe` below and run it. If you have 1.36 or newer, Capri updates herself. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed).

**Linux:** download `Capri-x86_64.AppImage` below, make it executable (right-click → Properties → Permissions → *Allow executing file as program*), and double-click it. If you have 1.37.0 or newer, it updates itself.

**What's in this one:**
- **Smoother everything.** Her animations now run on precise frame timers (Windows' normal timers tick in uneven 15 ms steps, which made motion judder), at a higher frame rate, and the full-screen effects only repaint what changes.
- **Teleporting.** Roaming is now a teleport: her power closes in around her, Blackwall-style - a dark void, jagged black-and-red shards spiralling up, red static - she tears apart and reappears somewhere else.
- **Bouncier slams.** Throw her into a wall: a split-second freeze, a white impact frame, the wall cracks and shatters, sparks and a shockwave - and she bounces off, tumbling, bounces along the floor and gets up with a spring.
- **Drag her** and she dangles from your cursor, swinging behind the way you move, legs kicking, glitching a little.
- **Real glitches** every few minutes, like a broken video feed: tearing, colour split, a frozen frame, a dropped frame. Settings → Extras → *She glitches* (or never).
- **Neon look** (the new default): her body dark, with glowing red and green edges. Settings → Character → Body to switch back to full colour.
- **Text box your way:** always there, only popping up when she talks back, or never. Settings → Extras → *Text box*.
- **No more opening screen:** everything it offered is in Settings → Extras.
- If she ever crashes, `crash.log` and `capri.log` now say why.
