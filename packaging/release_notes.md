**Download `Capri-Setup-1.36.1.exe` below and run it.** It installs for your user, with no admin rights or Python needed (Windows 10/11). If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed). If you already have 1.36.0, Capri updates herself.

**New in 1.36.1:**
- **Pick your AI model from a list.** Settings → Assistant → AI → **Show my models** lists the models your key can actually use (Ollama too), so there's no guessing names.
- **"Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite doesn't exist" is fixed.** When the AI service says a model doesn't exist (retired or renamed), Capri switches to the closest one it has and answers anyway. The Gemini default now follows Google's newest Flash model.

Tip: a Gemini key from https://aistudio.google.com/apikey has a free tier. A Gemini app subscription (Gemini Pro/Advanced) is separate from the API.
