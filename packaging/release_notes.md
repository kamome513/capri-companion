**Windows:** download `Capri-Setup-1.37.2.exe` below and run it. If you have 1.36 or newer, Capri updates herself. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed).

**Linux:** download `Capri-x86_64.AppImage` below, make it executable (right-click → Properties → Permissions → *Allow executing file as program*), and double-click it. If you have 1.37.0 or newer, it updates itself.

**New in 1.37.2: Capri shows up on Discord.** While she's open, your profile says **Playing Capri**, with her shard, "Plugged in", how long she's been on, and two buttons for your friends: **Get Capri** and **Join her Discord**.
- It only shows that Capri is open (and "Drone mode" while you game), never what you say to her or what you do.
- Turn it off: Settings → PC & chat → Discord. Discord's own Activity Privacy setting also applies.
- Works with the Discord app on Windows and Linux (including Flatpak and Snap).
