**Windows:** download `Capri-Setup-1.39.0.exe` below and run it. If you have 1.36 or newer, Capri updates herself. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway** (the installer isn't code-signed).

**Linux:** download `Capri-x86_64.AppImage` below, make it executable (right-click → Properties → Permissions → *Allow executing file as program*), and double-click it. If you have 1.37.0 or newer, it updates itself.

**What's in this one:**
- **Throw her.** Flick her and let go: she flies. Hit the edge of the screen hard and she slams into it, glitches, cracks the "wall", sees stars, slides down and gets back up.
- **She roams.** Now and then she walks somewhere else along the bottom of your screen, stepping one leg at a time. Say "stay there" to stop, "go for a walk" to send her.
- **"Hey Capri, clip that."** Turn on *Clip my games* (Settings → PC & chat) and she keeps the last two minutes of your game; "clip that" or "clip the last minute" saves it as a video. Optional: *Save big moments* clips triple kills, aces and wins by itself.
- **She watches your screen.** "What do you see?" / "look at my screen" any time, or turn on *Watch my screen* and she comments every few minutes. Needs an AI that can see pictures.
- **Her voice in your mic.** Pick a virtual cable in Settings and your Discord / Roblox friends hear her.
- **Head pats.** Click her head.
- **New voices and brains:** Fish Audio voices (free credits to start) and NVIDIA API keys (build.nvidia.com).
- On first start she shows *Extras*, where you can switch these on. All of it is in Settings too.
