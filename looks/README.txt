Your own looks for Capri.

Drop pictures of her in here (png / webp / jpg) and restart Capri - each one shows up in
Settings -> Character -> Style as "Look: <file name>", animated: hair that sways and
sparkles, blinking eyes (found by their blue irises), a grin that opens as she talks,
breathing, and the odd glitch.

A white background is cut out automatically (everything white that touches the edge), so
she stands free; a full picture is shown in a neon card instead.
