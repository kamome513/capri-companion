# Games

## Game hype ("nice kill!")

Settings → **PC & chat** → **Game hype**.
- **Cheer when I get kills:** Counter-Strike 2, Dota 2 and League of Legends report kills to her directly.
  For CS2 / Dota 2 she adds a small config file to the game's folder; restart the game once.
- **Any game: watch my screen for kills:** she reads "ELIMINATED", "HEADSHOT", "VICTORY"... off your screen.
  That's read on your PC and never sent anywhere.
- **Also say something when I die** and **Encourage me now and then** are there too.

## Clips ("hey Capri, clip that")

Settings → **Extras** → **Clips** → tick **Clip my games**. She keeps the last couple of minutes of your
screen and sound on your PC. Say "clip that" (or "clip the last minute") and she saves it as a video in
**Videos → Capri Clips**. Nothing is uploaded.

## "What do you see?"

Ask her about your screen any time: "Hey Capri, what do you see?" / "what's this?". Or let her comment by
herself: Settings → **Extras** → **Watch my screen**. This needs an AI that can see pictures (see
[Set up her AI](ai.md); with Ollama, say "give yourself eyes" first).

If your game shows up **black** to her, switch the game to **borderless** / **windowed fullscreen** in its
video settings. Exclusive fullscreen hides the picture from other apps.

## Let her play ("play this for me")

Settings → **Extras** → tick **Let her play games**. Then say "Hey Capri, play this for me". She looks at the
game in front and presses keys and clicks in it (only game keys, only in that game). **Esc stops her at once.**
It needs an AI that can see pictures.

## In-game

- While you're in a game she stays in her corner and gives memory back to the game (her bigger hearing
  model rests until you're done; she still hears "Hey Capri").
- "Why is my game lagging?" checks your CPU, memory, graphics card and disk and says what's slowing it down.
- "How's my internet?" checks your ping and download speed.
