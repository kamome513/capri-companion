# Discord

## "Mute me" / "deafen me"

Capri presses Discord's own **Toggle Mute** / **Toggle Deafen** keybinds for you.

1. In Discord: **User Settings** → **Keybinds** → **Add a Keybind** → *Toggle Mute* (and one for *Toggle
   Deafen*). Pick keys you don't use in games, e.g. `Ctrl+Shift+M` and `Ctrl+Shift+D`.
2. In Capri: Settings → **PC & chat** → **Discord** → type the same keys (empty = `Ctrl+Shift+M` /
   `Ctrl+Shift+D`).
3. Say "Hey Capri, mute me" / "unmute me" / "deafen me".

Don't run Discord "as administrator": Windows then blocks other apps from pressing its keys.

## "Join call" / "leave call"

1. In Discord, right-click the voice channel you use → **Copy Link**.
2. Settings → **PC & chat** → **Discord call** → paste it into **Your voice channel**. **Join it now** tests it.
3. Say "Hey Capri, join call". She opens the channel and presses **Join Voice**.
4. "Leave call": works best with a keybind. In Discord, Keybinds → *Disconnect from Voice Channel*, then type the
   same keys into **Leave call keybind**.

## "Stream my screen" (Windows)

While you're in a voice channel, say "Hey Capri, stream my screen" (or "stop streaming"). She clicks Discord's
share button for you.

## Your Discord status

With Settings → **PC & chat** → **Show "Playing Capri" on my Discord profile** ticked, your friends see:
- "Playing Capri" while she's open,
- that you're vibing to music (never which song),
- "Playing <your game> with Capri" while you game.

Nothing else is shown. The Discord app has to be running on the same PC. Discord's own
**Activity Privacy** setting still decides who can see it.

## Her voice in your Discord mic

So your friends hear her in calls:
1. Install [VB-Audio Virtual Cable](https://vb-audio.com/Cable/) (free).
2. Settings → **Extras** → **Her voice in your mic** → **Also play her voice on** → *CABLE Input*.
3. In Discord, set your input device to *CABLE Output*.

That cable carries only her voice, not yours. To send both at once, mix them with the free
[VoiceMeeter](https://vb-audio.com/Voicemeeter/) and pick its output as your Discord microphone.
