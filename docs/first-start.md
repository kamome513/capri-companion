# First start

## Talk to her

Say **"Hey Capri"**, wait for her to look up (her text box shows she's listening), then say what you want.
You can also say it all in one go: **"Hey Capri, open Discord"**.

Some things to try:
- "Hey Capri, open Chrome" / "close Spotify" / "open Fortnite"
- "Hey Capri, play some lo-fi" / "pause" / "next song" / "turn it up"
- "Hey Capri, what's the weather?" / "what time is it?"
- "Hey Capri, take a screenshot"
- "Hey Capri, set a timer for 10 minutes" / "remind me tomorrow at 9 to call mum"
- "Hey Capri, let's play rock paper scissors"
- "Hey Capri, put Chrome on the left and Discord on the right"
- "Hey Capri, how's my internet?" / "why is my game lagging?"

No mic, or you'd rather type? Right-click her → **Chat with Capri...**, or **Ctrl+Alt+T** from anywhere.
Push-to-talk: **Ctrl+Alt+C** makes her listen right away.

**Drop a file on her** (a text file, Word document, PDF or picture) and she tells you what's in it (needs her AI).

## Hotkeys

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| Ctrl+Alt+C | She listens for one command |
| Ctrl+Alt+T | The chat box |
| Ctrl+Alt+H | Hide / show her |
| Ctrl+Alt+P | Quiet mode: she doesn't comment by herself, but still answers you |
| Ctrl+Alt+Shift+C | Click-through on / off |

## If she doesn't hear you

1. Right-click her → **Voice** → **Microphone** and pick the mic you talk into.
2. Windows: Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone → allow desktop apps.
3. Settings → **Wake word** → **Teach her your voice** (say "Hey Capri" a few times), or lower
   **Wake word confidence**.
4. Strong accent? Settings → **Wake word** → **Hearing** → **Parakeet** (a better speech model, downloaded once).

## Her look and size

- Right-click her → **Size** → **Bigger** / **Smaller**. Her text box grows and shrinks with her.
- Right-click her → **Panel** → **Text box opens** → *Always*, *When she has something to say*, *Only when you
  say her name*, or *Never*.
- Drag her anywhere. Right-click → **Reset position** puts her back in the corner.
- Settings → **Character** has her style, hair and colours.

## Her voice

She speaks with a voice on your PC. For her proper neural voice: Settings → **Assistant** → **Download
Capri's voice** (120 MB, once). Online voices: see [ElevenLabs](voice-elevenlabs.md) and
[Fish Audio](voice-fish-audio.md).

## Chatting with her

Commands work without any AI. To chat with her about anything, give her an AI: see [Set up her AI](ai.md).

## Settings

Right-click her → **Settings...**. Everything you change is saved when you close the window.

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| Apps / Found on this PC | The apps and games she opens, and what you call them |
| Wake word | Language, mic clean-up, hearing model, hotkeys |
| Character | Her style, hair, colours |
| Assistant | Her voice, personality, weather, music, AI |
| PC & chat | Greeting, game hype, Discord, updates, power |
| Extras | Text box, clips, watching your screen, throwing her, music deck, letting her play |
