# Languages

Capri understands and speaks 25 languages: Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Estonian,
Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese,
Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish and Ukrainian.

## Switch language

Any of these:
- Right-click her → **Language** → pick yours.
- Settings → **Wake word** → **Language**.
- Say "Hey Capri, speak German" / "switch to French".

The first time, she downloads the Parakeet v3 speech model (about 490 MB, once). It runs on your PC.
You still wake her with **"Hey Capri"**.

## What works

- **Everyday commands work by themselves** in every language: open and close apps, play, pause, next,
  previous, louder, quieter, the time, the weather, screenshots.
- **With an AI** ([set one up](ai.md)) she understands everything you say and answers in your language. Her
  own lines ("Opened Discord.") are translated the first time and remembered on your PC.

## Her voice in your language

- **Her own neural voice** (Settings → Assistant → Download Capri's voice) speaks all 25 languages with
  each language's pronunciation.
- Without it, she uses a Windows voice for that language if you have one (Windows Settings → Time &
  language → Speech → Add voices), or espeak-ng.
- **ElevenLabs** and **Fish Audio** voices speak them all.

**Only want to change what she hears?** Tick Settings → **Wake word** → **Keep her voice when I change
language**. Then her voice stays exactly as you picked it.
